Our Story

Thirty-five years of honest baking

In 1991, in the industrial quarter of Pokhara, a small team lit the first ovens of what would become Pokhara Foods. The promise was simple: biscuits made from good ingredients, baked properly, priced for every family.

Three decades on, the Bakeland name sits on shelves in more than fifty districts. We still source wheat we can trace, still check every batch, and still believe that a biscuit bought for a child's tiffin should never be a compromise.

"We don't chase trends. We bake what families trust."

The Craft

From field to family

Five honest steps stand between a wheat field and your tea table — each one checked by people who eat what they bake.

01 — Sourced

Wheat we can trace

Domestic wheat and quality flour, inspected at intake — Nepal grows about 2.1 million tonnes a year, and we start there.

02 — Baked

The slow bake

Dough is rested, sheeted and baked to a golden standard on modern lines — never rushed to the oven.

03 — Checked

Every batch, every bite

Quality control at every critical stage — taste, texture, bite and shelf life, verified before anything ships.

04 — Sealed

Freshness locked in

Hygienic packaging with clear labelling keeps every pack crunchy from the factory line to the home.

05 — Delivered

To a shelf near you

Route vans, distributors and retail partners carry Bakeland nationwide — reliably, and on time.
